Large-scale structured optimization

Topics ↑

Let structure do the heavy lifting. I exploit sparsity, low rank, and conic geometry to solve optimization problems at scale. Practical inexact optimization

Topics ↑

How accurately do we really need to solve each subproblem? I develop implementable stopping rules and convergence theory that let algorithms save work through controlled inexactness. This connects proximal and augmented Lagrangian methods with decentralized optimization and distributionally robust learning.

ripALM · simpler error control

One relative tolerance

A single relative-error tolerance controls inexact subproblem solves, without correction steps or a prescribed summable tolerance sequence.

Global convergence, with asymptotic linear or superlinear rates under additional assumptions. Read ripALM.

Optimal transport & distributional geometry

Topics ↑

Compare distributions without sacrificing accuracy for scale. I build transport solvers around sparse Newton steps, proximal regularization, and verifiable inexact solves. These ideas extend from discrete and structured transport to Gromov–Wasserstein problems, where the goal is to align the internal geometry of different spaces.

Optimal experimental design

Topics ↑

Choose the data before collecting it. I develop methods for allocating limited experimental resources where they are most informative. Newton and vertex-exchange algorithms make classical design problems tractable, while geometry-aware probability metrics offer more stable objectives for Bayesian experimental design.

Optimization & AI

Topics ↑

Use optimization to understand learning—and learning to improve optimization. My work connects provable algorithms for reinforcement learning with learned algorithm parameters, LLM agents that formulate and solve optimization problems, and symbolic discovery that makes the structure of differential equations more interpretable.

Certifiable optimization for robotics

Topics ↑

A good trajectory is more useful when we know how good it is. I combine semidefinite relaxations, safeguarded local search, and GPU computation to tackle nonconvex problems in robotics. The aim is to deliver practical solutions together with computable bounds on their distance from global optimality.
